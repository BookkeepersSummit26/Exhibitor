About the Event

Event Information

Everything you need to know about the Bookkeepers Summit 2026 and your audience.

The Summit

The World's Largest Gathering of Bookkeepers

ICB's flagship annual event, held each November in London during Global Bookkeeping Week.

The Summit brings together professional bookkeepers and the businesses that serve them for a packed day of learning, insight and inspiration. As a confirmed exhibitor, your stand is positioned directly next to the main hall, with dedicated networking breaks driving consistent, high-quality footfall throughout the day.

The 2026 event runs across 16 November at the QEII Centre in Westminster — one of London's most prestigious conference venues, steps from Parliament Square. In-person attendees are joined by a large virtual audience via the Summit app and online platform.

All exhibitors benefit from the Fever Pitch session — 60 seconds on the main plenary stage to deliver your elevator pitch to every delegate in the room. Speaker details are due by Friday 2 October.

The event concludes with the prestigious ICB LUCA Awards, celebrating excellence across the profession. Explore category sponsorship →

🎨 Conference Expo — Stand Graphics & Setup

Once your profile has been completed, we will set up your profile on the Conference Expo stand portal so you can upload your graphics and approve your stand.

You will receive an email from portal@conferenceexpo.co.uk with the subject line "Account created for the Conference Expo portal" — please check your junk/spam if you have not received it.

Once you have accessed the portal, please review each tab and follow the steps to confirm your details, select any upgrades and upload your artwork for approval. You will be able to download a graphic template for the backwall and counter specific to your stand size.

⏰ Stand extras deadline: The deadline for ordering any additional items and uploading your artwork is 5pm on Friday 31 October. We recommend uploading prior to this date in case you need any extra time to make changes.
Questions about additional items or graphics? Contact portal@conferenceexpo.co.uk

Stand extras available include: plasma screens, poseur tables, stools, literature racks. Note: plasma screens must be ordered before you upload your artwork as this changes the graphic template provided.

🎙️ In-Person 20-Minute Workshop (if applicable to your package)

If your package includes a 20-minute workshop, we will require the name of speaker, job title and email address ASAP. Please send to info@bookkeeperssummit.com. We will then ask the speaker to complete a short survey to confirm their requirements for the session.

The workshops will be allocated based on the session details provided as to where they will be best placed in the programme and your slot will be confirmed as soon as possible on the programme online.

🎤 In-Person Summit Fever Pitch — Monday 16 November

Each sponsor gets 60 seconds to pitch why the delegates should visit their stand. It's a fast-paced, fun start to the day. Our advice would be to think about what you're going to say, practise, and time yourself!

Please send the speaker's name and job title to sobi.hariharan@bookkeepers.org.uk by Monday 19 October.

🎬 Pre-Recorded Demo for Virtual Summit — Wednesday 25 November (if applicable)

For our virtual tech day, we're looking for a pure demos of your product. This will be a 20-minute pre-recorded demo and we will require a name of speaker, email address, job title ASAP. The pre-record should be recorded horizontally/landscape and be a max of 20 minutes and sent to info@bookkeeperssummit.com by Friday 31 October.

📹 Tips for recording a good quality video:

📦 Delivery Instructions

Deliveries are permitted on Friday 13 November between 07:00–19:00 only, and all deliveries should be made at the Loading Bay on Storey's Gate Road. The Loading Bay can accommodate vehicles up to 3.5 metres wide and there are no height restrictions. The loading bay is located on the ground floor of the QEII Centre.

All deliveries should have a completed delivery note attached to all items, and all items being left for collection post-event need to have a completed collection note attached.

If you are expecting goods to be delivered by courier, please ensure they are aware of the dates of the show, as any deliveries made prior to Friday 13 November may not be accepted as there may be other events taking place.

The QEII Centre does not allow load in/out via the front doors during set-up or break-down, unless it is something small that can be carried in a bag or box and walked into the building.

🛡️ Third-Party Sexual Harassment Clause

ICB is dedicated to ensuring a safe and respectful working environment for all employees and will not tolerate any form of sexual harassment.

Our full Sexual Harassment Policy is available on request — you are expected to read it carefully and fully understand it.

Please be aware that any violation of our Sexual Harassment Policy will lead to the following actions, depending on the severity of the incident:

  1. Warning and potential service termination: if your behaviour is determined as a breach of our Sexual Harassment Policy, you will receive a formal warning and be notified that further violations will result in the termination of our contract with you. Should there be any repeated instances, we will terminate/revoke our contract with you.
  2. Immediate removal from premises: in situations involving serious breaches, you may be required to leave our premises immediately.
  3. Immediate termination of services: for serious breaches, ICB will terminate our contract with you with immediate effect.

If your actions are considered criminal, they will be reported to the relevant authorities. We may also share relevant details of the incident with our global partners to ensure a unified and co-ordinated response across the organisation.

ICB reserves the right to enforce any of these measures based on the specifics of the incident. By providing services to us, you agree to adhere to our policies and contribute to maintaining a respectful, harassment-free environment.
